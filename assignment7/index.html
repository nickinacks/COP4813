Card Dealer and Discard Game

How to Play

Click Deal Cards to receive five random playing cards from a standard 52-card deck.

Drag any card from your hand into the Discard Pile to remove it. A confirmation message will appear when the card is successfully discarded.

Click Deal Cards again to start a new hand.
